Privacy Policy

How we protect and manage your data.

Effective Date: May 14, 2026

Welcome to May Learning Hub ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website (maylearninghub.co.za) or use the May Learning Hub Android application available on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the information absolutely necessary to provide you with our educational platform. When you create an account, register for activities, or fill out our feedback forms, we may ask for:

  • Personal Identifiable Information: Your Name and Email Address.
  • Educational Information: The name of your School.

2. How We Use Your Information

The minimal data we collect is used strictly to enhance your educational experience:

  • To create and secure your user account.
  • To verify that you are a student, educator, or parent interacting with our CAPS-aligned materials.
  • To respond to your feedback, support queries, or requests for specific presentations, games, and learning guides.

3. Third-Party Analytics

To understand how users interact with our site and app, and to help us improve our content, we use Google Analytics. This service tracks basic, non-personally identifiable user traffic data (such as pages visited, time spent on the app, and general geographic region). Google Analytics has its own privacy practices, which you can review in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties. Your data is kept strictly confidential.

We implement standard security measures, including Firebase Authentication, to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Children's Privacy

Because our platform is an educational tool built for learners (Grades 8-12), we take extra care. We strictly limit data collection to a name, email, and school to facilitate learning. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have inadvertently collected excessive personal information from your child, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app features or legal requirements. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date".

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to the developer, MJ Mayaya, using the Feedback Form on our homepage.
