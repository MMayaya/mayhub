Privacy Policy

How we protect and manage your data.

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

May Learning Hub respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use maylearninghub.co.za, the May Learning Hub Android application, or the May Exam & Invigilation Manager Windows application. May Learning Hub is operated by MJ Mayaya and processes personal information in accordance with applicable South African law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Information We Collect

We collect only information reasonably needed to provide and support our services. This may include:

  • your name, email address, school or organisation;
  • information you include in feedback or support requests;
  • licence information, such as an activation code, app version, licence status, device identifier, device limit, and expiry date; and
  • website usage and device information collected through Google Analytics, such as pages visited, browser type, general location, and visit times.

The Windows app does not upload the exam timetables, invigilation schedules, teacher lists, or documents you create. These remain on your computer unless you choose to export or share them.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide educational content, app features, and customer support;
  • receive and approve licence registrations;
  • activate licences and enforce device limits and expiry dates;
  • prevent invalid-code guessing, fraud, and misuse;
  • understand and improve the website and applications; and
  • meet legal obligations and protect our users and services.

3. Licence Approval

The Windows app checks whether an activation code is valid, available for use, within its device limit, and not expired. Valid new registrations must still be approved online by May Learning Hub before the full version becomes active. If you believe a licence decision is incorrect, please contact us for assistance.

4. Google Services and Data Sharing

May Learning Hub uses Google Analytics and Firebase/Google Cloud to provide website analytics and the Windows licensing service. Google may process information needed to operate and secure these services, including technical and network information. Information may be processed in South Africa or other countries where Google and its service providers operate. You can learn more from Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the services, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users, rights, and security.

5. Data Retention and Security

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, manage licences, answer support requests, prevent misuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. Temporary security records used to limit repeated activation attempts are automatically deleted after a short period. Information that is no longer required is deleted or de-identified where reasonably possible.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, including secure internet connections, restricted access, protected licence records, and controls against repeated activation attempts. No electronic service can be guaranteed completely secure, so users should also protect their devices, accounts, passwords, and activation codes.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to POPIA, you may ask whether we hold personal information about you and request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. You may also object to certain processing or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

To make a privacy request, email MJMayaya@gmail.com or use the Feedback Form. If you are not satisfied with the response, you may contact the South African Information Regulator through its official eServices portal.

7. Children's Privacy

May Learning Hub provides educational material that may be used by learners. The Windows licence registration should be completed by the purchaser, teacher, school representative, parent, guardian, or another responsible adult. Learners' personal details should not be submitted for licence registration unless this is necessary and properly authorised. Please contact us if you believe a child's personal information was provided improperly.

8. Changes and Contact

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

For general questions or concerns about this Policy, use the Feedback Form.
