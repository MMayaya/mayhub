Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install the app?

Because the app is currently in its testing phase, you must first send us your email address. Once approved, click the Google Play button above to download and install the app safely from the official store.

Is the May Hub App available for iOS/iPhones?

Currently, the app is optimized exclusively for Android devices. iOS users can continue to use the web version seamlessly.

When should I message via WhatsApp support?

You may contact us via WhatsApp if you experience any issues while using the app or website, such as broken download links, pages not opening properly, login problems, or other technical difficulties. Educators and users are also welcome to reach out if they notice any information in our content that may be incorrect, outdated, unclear, or misleading. We appreciate helpful feedback, as it helps us improve the quality and accuracy of the learning materials.

While we may try to guide learners where possible, schoolwork questions, homework help, and detailed subject tutoring may not always be answered through this channel.
