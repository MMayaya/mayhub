Make Your Practice Count

Three habits for Mathematics revision

  1. 1. Understand the method

    Review a presentation or worked example before tackling a new type of question.

  2. 2. Work it out yourself

    Keep a pen and paper nearby. Write down each step before choosing or revealing an answer.

  3. 3. Learn from each attempt

    Check your signs, powers and calculations. Revisit difficult questions and explain your method in your own words.
